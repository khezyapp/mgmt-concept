# 04 — New Document SOP: What to Do Without Being Told

Use when any of these land in your inbox, Telegram group, or shared drive:

- a new or revised strategy, sub-decree, prakas, policy, or national plan
- a ministry instruction, circular, or official letter
- a new technical standard, security baseline, or data-sharing rule
- a donor requirement, appraisal mission aide-mémoire, or reporting template
- meeting minutes that assign an action to your unit
- an update to an existing document (new version, annex, or timeline)

Goal: in **60 minutes**, know whether it affects you and what you will do — without
waiting for a briefing.

---

## 1. Principle: triage, not full reading

You do not need to read 60 pages to decide what to do. You need five answers:

1. What type of document is it?
2. Who owns it, and does it name us?
3. What obligations or targets does it create?
4. What does it change for our policy, system, process, budget, or reporting?
5. What is my response, and by when?

Read only the sections that answer those questions. Full reading comes later, and only
if the document passes the relevance gates.

---

## 2. The 60-minute workflow

| Time | Step | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | **Log and classify** | Document card: title, issuer, date, version, type, status, owner |
| 10–25 | **Extract** | Obligations, numbers, named actors, dates, reporting duties |
| 25–30 | **Relevance gates** | In / out decision with a reason |
| 30–40 | **Impact analysis** | Changes needed in policy, standard, procedure, system, backlog, budget |
| 40–45 | **Choose response mode** | Ignore/record, watch, comply, contribute, or propose |
| 45–60 | **Produce and send** | Triage note, tasks, one-paragraph message, calendar entries |

If the document is clearly irrelevant, stop at 15 minutes and record why. That record
protects you later.

---

## 3. Step 1 — Log and classify

Create a row in your document register:

```
Document ID:      DOC-2026-014
Title:            [exact title]
Issuer:           [ministry / unit / donor]
Date / version:   [issue date, version, effective date]
Type:             strategy | policy | standard | procedure | guideline | plan | instruction
Status:           draft | approved | effective | superseded
Owner:            [named unit / official]
Scope:            [who and what]
Source:           [URL / file path]
```

**Classification shortcut:** look at the verbs and the structure.

| Signal | Type |
|---|---|
| Vision, objectives, pillars, 3–5 year horizon | Strategy |
| Shall/must, mandatory, compliance, roles, exceptions | Policy |
| Thresholds, formats, schemas, "conform to", test criteria | Standard |
| Numbered steps, inputs/outputs, roles per step | Procedure |
| "Recommended", "should consider", examples, options | Guideline |
| Tasks, owners, dates, budget, deliverables | Action plan |

If it mixes types (common), split it: "this document contains a policy (sections 1–3)
and an action plan (annex 2)." Treat each part separately.

---

## 4. Step 2 — Extract seven things

Read with a highlighter and fill this table:

| # | Extract | Questions | Where to look |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purpose | Why was this issued now? | Preamble, first section |
| 2 | Scope | Who/what is in and out? | Early definitions; "application" section |
| 3 | Obligations | Every shall/must/ensure/prohibit | Body; use the verb decoder (01) |
| 4 | Numbers | Targets, baselines, dates, budgets | Objectives, annexes, tables |
| 5 | Actors | Who is named responsible? Us? | Annex, signature block, roles section |
| 6 | Deadlines | Effective date, reporting dates, milestones | Timeline, annex, M&E section |
| 7 | Change | What is new vs the previous version? | Compare versions; look for "amend", "replace" |

For a long document, extract only sections matching your domain (finance, credit,
guarantee, data, digital, reporting). Ignore the rest until needed.

---

## 5. Step 3 — Relevance gates

Answer four questions. Any "yes" means relevant; all "no" means record and stop.

```
1. Does it name my unit, my mandate, or a system I own?        yes → relevant
2. Does it change a rule I must follow or enforce?             yes → relevant
3. Does it require data, reports, or integration from us?      yes → relevant
4. Does it create a target we are expected to contribute to?   yes → relevant
```

If relevant but not owned by you, choose **contribute** rather than **propose**.
If relevant and owned by you, go to [05-proposal-playbook.md](05-proposal-playbook.md).

**Delegation check:** a document may name a ministry while the actual work sits with a
unit. Confirm delegation in writing before you claim ownership. Absence of your name is
not absence of duty — but it is absence of authority.

---

## 6. Step 4 — Impact analysis

Check six surfaces. Most people only check the first one.

| Surface | Ask | Typical output |
|---|---|---|
| Policy | Does our policy conflict or need a new clause? | Policy amendment draft |
| Standard | Do our technical specs meet the new rule? | Gap assessment, standard update |
| Procedure | Does our SOP still work? | Revised steps, new roles, new records |
| System | What must change in data, API, UI, reports? | Backlog items, architecture decision |
| Reporting / M&E | What must we report, to whom, how often? | Indicator definition, report calendar |
| Budget / people | What resources are needed? | Cost estimate, training plan |

Impact scale:

| Scale | Meaning | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Structural** | Changes mandate, law, or system architecture | Escalate; full proposal and steering decision |
| **B — Operational** | Changes process, reports, or a module | Plan and schedule within the quarter |
| **C — Informational** | No action now; monitor | Log and set a review date |

---

## 7. Step 5 — Choose a response mode

```
                    Is it relevant?
                    /            \
                  no              yes
                  │                │
          Log & stop        Do we own the duty?
          (5 min)           /            \
                          no              yes
                          │                │
                   Contribute or      Can we deliver
                      watch?          with current capacity?
                      /      \          /          \
                   watch  contribute   yes          no
                    │         │         │            │
                 calendar   offer     propose      propose phased
                 review     support   & execute    plan / escalate
```

| Mode | When | What you produce |
|---|---|---|
| **Record** | Out of scope, no duty | One-line log entry with reason |
| **Watch** | Future relevance, no current duty | Calendar review date; no work now |
| **Comply** | Clear obligation, existing owner and process | Gap check, fix, evidence |
| **Contribute** | Relevant, but another unit leads | Offer specific input: data, API, standard clause, review |
| **Propose** | Relevant and within your mandate | Concept note / one-pager (05) |

**Never** choose "wait for instructions" as a mode. If you are unsure, choose **watch**
with a review date and send one clarifying question with a recommended default.

---

## 8. Step 6 — Produce and send

Send a short message, not a book:

> **Subject:** [Document] — impact and proposed response
>
> **What it is:** [type, issuer, date].
> **What changed:** [one sentence].
> **Why it matters to us:** [obligation/number/deadline].
> **Impact:** [policy/system/reporting], scale A/B/C.
> **Proposed response:** [mode] — [one action], owner [name], by [date].
> **Assumption:** [one line], to confirm by [date].
> **Attachment:** triage card.

Then create the tasks. A document with no task is not implemented.

---

## 9. Trigger table: if the document says X, do Y

| If it says... | Then... |
|---|---|
| "shall/must" + your domain | Add a compliance item; map to a control and a test |
| A target percentage or count | Define the indicator, baseline, data source, and reporting frequency |
| "lead" / "responsible" + your unit | Confirm mandate and budget; start a concept note |
| "coordinate" / "participate" | Offer a specific contribution; do not take ownership |
| "by Q_ 20__" | Work backwards; add milestones and dependencies to the calendar |
| "report" / "submit" | Add to the reporting calendar; assign a data owner |
| "develop a system/portal/platform" | Start with process and data mapping before architecture |
| "may" / "encourage" / "promote" | Treat as optional unless tied to a target or budget line |
| "amend" / "replace" | Diff against the old version; list changed clauses and impact |
| "effective from" | Check the compliance date; plan retrofits if the system is live |

## 10. Be ready before the document arrives

Triage is fast only if your baseline is current. Keep these seven artifacts in one
folder; update them quarterly or after any major change.

| Artifact | Contents | Why it saves time |
|---|---|---|
| **Mandate card** | Legal basis, functions, delegations, who can approve what | Answers "is this ours?" in one minute |
| **System and data map** | Services, APIs, databases, data owners, integrations | Answers "what changes?" without asking engineers |
| **Process map** | Core workflows, step owners, systems, records | Shows where a new rule enters the workflow |
| **Indicator sheet** | Indicators you report, definitions, sources, baselines | Lets you map a new target to existing data |
| **Reporting calendar** | Every recurring report, owner, due date | Prevents late discovery of a new deadline |
| **Obligation register** | Policies, standards, and laws you must comply with | Lets you diff a new document against current duties |
| **Partner list** | Banks, ministries, vendors, agreements, contacts | Identifies dependencies and data-sharing constraints |

If an artifact is missing, that is your first task after reading the new document — not
a reason to wait.

---

## 11. First 24 hours / first week

**First 24 hours**
- [ ] Log the document and assign an ID
- [ ] Classify the type and status (draft vs effective)
- [ ] Check whether your unit is named
- [ ] Identify the top three obligations or numbers
- [ ] Send the one-paragraph impact note with a proposed response

**First week**
- [ ] Complete the impact analysis across six surfaces
- [ ] Add or update register rows (key action, KPI, task)
- [ ] Put reporting deadlines in the calendar
- [ ] If scale A/B, draft the concept note or gap plan
- [ ] Confirm assumptions with one precise question
- [ ] Brief your manager in five minutes, with the ask

## 12. Reporting calendar anchors (MSME Strategy)

From §6.2.1.4, p. 33–34:

| Report | Due |
|---|---|
| Semi-annual M&E report | Within 1 month after the end of each half-year |
| Annual M&E report | Within 2 months after the end of each fiscal year |
| Mid-term report | By Q2 2028 |
| End-term report | By Q2 2030 |

Put these in your calendar now. Work backwards: data cut-off, validation, internal
review, sign-off, submission. The Secretariat consolidates after receiving your report.

## 13. Worked mini-example

**Trigger:** MEF issues an instruction requiring monthly reporting of guaranteed loans
by partner bank and MSME size.

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Log | `DOC-2026-021`, MEF instruction, effective 2026-12-01, type = policy + reporting requirement |
| Extract | Obligation: monthly report; data: bank, loan value, MSME size, guarantee status; due: 10th of following month; owner: MEF |
| Relevance | Yes: we operate the guarantee workflow and hold the data |
| Impact | System: new report view + data completeness rules; Procedure: monthly close step; Standard: data definitions; Reporting: calendar entry |
| Mode | Comply + propose a small enhancement |
| Output | Gap note: 3 data fields missing; `TSK-REP-004` to add fields; `TSK-REP-005` to automate the report; owner and dates; assumption that historical data is not required |

Notice: the instruction did not say "build a report". The translation from obligation to
system change is the professional work.

---

## 14. Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Cost | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Reading everything before deciding | Lost days; late response | Triage with the seven extracts first |
| Forwarding the document with "FYI" | No action; work lands on you later | Send impact + proposed response |
| Waiting for a meeting | You lose the initiative | Send assumption + default + date |
| Treating a draft as effective | Premature work; rework | Check status; plan but do not commit budget |
| Treating "promote" as "build" | Scope creep | Separate influence from delivery |
| Owning everything relevant | Overload; weak delivery | Use contribute mode where another unit leads |
| No evidence | Audit finding | Define evidence at task creation |
